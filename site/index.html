How we verify

  1. Source registry. We track a list of official funding pages, most of which reopen every year, and check them for changes daily.
  2. Discovery. Aggregators and conference sites are used only to find new programmes, never as the source we cite.
  3. Extraction. Deadline, amount, eligibility and payment method are copied into fixed fields from the official page.
  4. Verification. A second pass reopens the official page. If a field can't be confirmed it shows "not stated", never a guess.
  5. Human check. Every week a sample of listings is checked by hand and the error rates are published below.
